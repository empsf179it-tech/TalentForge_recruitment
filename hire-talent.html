Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our recruitment process.

What is your typical placement timeframe?

Our average time-to-hire is between 14 to 21 days depending on the complexity of the role and industry requirements. Executive roles may take slightly longer due to extensive vetting.

Do you offer international recruitment services?

Yes, we have a global network and specialize in sourcing top-tier talent from around the world for remote, hybrid, and relocation roles across major global hubs.

What is your fee structure?

Our fee structure varies depending on the type of engagement (contingency, retained search, or contract staffing). Please contact us directly for a customized, transparent quote based on your needs.

How do you guarantee candidate quality?

We use a rigorous multi-stage vetting process that includes AI-driven resume screening, in-depth behavioral interviews, and comprehensive technical assessments tailored to the specific role.

What happens if a placement doesn't work out?

We offer an extended replacement guarantee. If a candidate leaves within the guarantee period, our team will immediately source and provide a suitable replacement at no additional cost to you.
